# Wearable Tiles

This is a quick reference to Riichi Mahjong tiles you may not be familiar with, designed for Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses.
It is a learning aid for the tiles that require you to learn Chinese characters to read; i.e. the man, wind, and dragon tiles.
With this open in your glasses, you can quickly check tile meanings while playing without having to look away from the table.
Besides tile meanings, it includes tile order for the purposes of determining dora in Riichi.

## Settings

Within the app you can configure

1. Zoom in or out: when checked, separate pages will be used for the set of man tiles and the winds and dragons together.
   Scroll left and right to switch between pages.
   When unchecked, all 17 tiles will be shown on the display at once.
